Observed anchor & sources

The earnings anchor is Korea's adjusted 2020 group means for full-time physicians; residents excluded. The 2026 starting distribution uses that relative earnings structure; it is not an observed 2026 income estimate.

Administrative remuneration is winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles. The proprietor weight is reconstructed from the same table’s group and overall means; it is not an observed workforce headcount share.

Clinical work is a modeled workload index. The two buildings and their illustrative actor slots depict work settings, not a census of clinic or hospital ownership.

    How to read the results

    Earnings indices track group means, including reduced or lost earnings. Retained positions and required clinical work are separate outcomes. The role earnings ratio compares the two group means. Care index is a weighted average of within-role activity indices.

    Two-point Gini under reconstructed weights; a lower bound only if the two means and weights describe one nonnegative eligible earnings population. Within-role inequality is unobserved.

    The solid and dashed paths hold the observed anchor fixed and vary model assumptions. They are conditional scenarios, without assigned forecast probabilities.
