# Research evidence and model use

Primary sources checked 2026-10-02; targeted Korean workflow and payment evidence added 2026-10-03. This is a bounded mechanism-oriented review, not a systematic review or meta-analysis. The empirical Korean earnings anchors and official workforce tables are documented in [baseline-sources.md](baseline-sources.md). Future workflow, staffing and distribution assumptions remain distinct from observed study effects.

The Korean documentation studies narrow the plausibility claim to a discharge-note subtask. Neither study identifies documentation's share of all physician labor, national AI adoption, additional completed visits, staffing displacement or wages. They predate the 2026 baseline, so their full contemporary deployment effects cannot automatically become incremental future gains. No Korean coefficient replaces the declared task-composition, demand, other-capacity, retention or earnings-capture sensitivity axes.

The consultation survey supports separating new and established cases and respecting institutional scheduling constraints. Its ideal task allocation is not an observed physician time budget. The fee-policy study supports separate payment and scheduling responses; its adjusted Saturday shares cannot calibrate a universal total-demand elasticity or individual-income pass-through. Clinic receipts, proprietor business income and employee compensation remain different quantities.

The targeted search checked Korean consultation-time surveys and original tables, ED documentation experiments and actual implementation, and payment/compensation response studies. The [2020 Korean Physician Survey report](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/library/10120/contents/5173879) was identified, but its original time-use tables were not retrieved. No usable nationally representative, mutually exclusive physician task-time shares, Korean AI effect on completed visits/total working time, or medical salaried wage/volume coefficient was retrieved. These are bounded retrieval gaps, not claims that such evidence does not exist; foreign values are not substituted as Korean coefficients.

## Consequences for the equations

Documentation, reasoning/planning, hands-on treatment and patient interaction have separate net time effects. Documentation savings are not labeled as removal of patient interaction. Reasoning gains can be zero or negative because more thorough review can improve quality while taking longer. Physical autonomy is a separate speculative scenario; no study here determines a year of autonomous Korean medical practice.

Current intervention-versus-comparator effects do not directly identify additional savings relative to a 2026 baseline that already contains some AI. The study effects serve as plausibility anchors. The separate trial-transport exercise applies ambient-scribe ITT estimates once under explicit documentation-share assumptions; encounter utilization is not multiplied in again. Overlapping documentation/EHR endpoints are not added.

Care output is constrained by demand, physician time and other care capacity. Saved time can therefore become spare work rather than proportionate throughput or job loss. The staffing response and earnings participation parameters remain assumptions because these trials do not estimate national physician displacement or role-specific profit capture.

## Economic framing and identification

The earlier project review used [Autor, Levy and Murnane](https://www.nber.org/papers/w8337) and [Acemoglu and Restrepo](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.33.2.3) for task substitution/complementarity and the distinction between productivity and labor demand. That review inspected abstracts and selected author/publisher passages, not a full equation-level replication. The current compensation envelope is an explicit modeling choice; it is not an estimated coefficient from those papers.

The Korean source audit supports two role means but no income-eligible role counts, within-role distribution or institution×role joint remuneration. The runtime therefore does not infer skill-ranked layoffs, physician ownership of every hospital, national Gini, or permanent closures from invented reserves. It tests whether group gaps change under declared assumptions.

## Reuse and provenance

`data/processed/ai-evidence.json` is the machine-readable workflow, consultation-time and payment evidence ledger with DOI/URL, design, unit, effect, retrieval scope and applicability. Arm-specific intervals, descriptive SDs, raw before/after shares and adjusted coefficients retain their different meanings. It contains limited factual results and original summaries; no full research-paper PDFs are redistributed. The empirical extraction ledger separately records raw-source hashes, exact workbook/PDF locators and work-specific reuse terms.
